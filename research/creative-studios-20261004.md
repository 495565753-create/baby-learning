## Browser audio implementation

User gestures resume AudioContext. Generation tokens cancel late asynchronous starts; leaving, muting and page hiding stop timers and notes. Volume defaults to 70%; compressor and soft limiter bound simultaneous notes. Numeric audio QA verifies signal level and no clipped samples, without claiming subjective human listening or physical iPhone speaker validation.

Official documentation checked:
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API/Best_practices
